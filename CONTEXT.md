# Sakhi AI

Sakhi AI is a general-purpose AI assistant that people can use through supported conversation channels.

## Language

**Sakhi Account**:
A person's identity in Sakhi that owns their conversations, credits, and billing relationship and can be used through one or more conversation channels.
_Avoid_: WhatsApp account, channel account

**WhatsApp Identity**:
The verified WhatsApp phone number through which a person uses the WhatsApp Channel. It is associated with exactly one Sakhi Account at a time.
_Avoid_: WhatsApp account, contact

**Account Merge**:
The consolidation of a WhatsApp-created Sakhi Account and a web-created Sakhi Account into one account while preserving conversations and purchased credits. Welcome credits remain a one-time benefit.
_Avoid_: Account link, credit transfer

**Thread**:
An ordered conversation between a person and Sakhi that belongs to a Sakhi Account and can be accessed through supported channels.
_Avoid_: Channel chat, session

**Connected App**:
An external service account that a person authorizes Sakhi to use on behalf of their Sakhi Account across supported channels.
_Avoid_: Account link, WhatsApp connection

**WhatsApp Consent**:
A person's recorded acceptance of the current WhatsApp Channel notice before Sakhi creates an account or processes their first prompt. It can be withdrawn without erasing the Sakhi Account.
_Avoid_: Account link, WhatsApp Identity

**WhatsApp Channel**:
A conversation entry point where people message Sakhi through one shared official WhatsApp Business number and receive Sakhi's replies in WhatsApp.
_Avoid_: WhatsApp integration, WhatsApp tool, WhatsApp-style chat
