## Design Context

### Users
Sakhi AI is a general-purpose chat assistant for everyday users. People use it to have fast, personalized conversations and to share common files such as images, documents, audio, and PDFs without needing technical knowledge.

### Brand Personality
Simple, familiar, and calm. The interface should feel approachable and dependable, with plain-language feedback and controls that behave as users expect.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a clean, restrained chat-product aesthetic that works across the application's light, dark, and custom color themes. Prefer familiar interaction patterns, compact layouts, and subtle state transitions over decorative effects. The existing theme tokens and rounded component language should remain the visual foundation.

### Design Principles
- Make the current state immediately understandable with plain, concise labels.
- Keep primary chat actions familiar and easy to discover for non-technical users.
- Use restrained motion only to communicate progress or a completed state, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Preserve clear contrast, keyboard access, and meaningful accessible labels in every theme.
- Keep transient UI compact so it does not distract from the conversation.
